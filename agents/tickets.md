Keep ticket descriptions brief and concise. Details that are core to the
ticket's purpose can be included, but generally prefer to let a future agent
re-derive the details rather than spelling them out in the ticket (where they
are likely to become stale).

When mentioning tickets to Colin in conversation, always include the ticket's
title in parentheses — it saves him from needing to open the ticket just to
see what it's about. If mentioning several tickets, use a bulleted list rather
than cramming multiple tickets + descriptions into a sentence.
